SMALL STEPS, BIG IMPACT

One world, one mission — end plastic pollution.

Simple lifestyle changes to cut down on plastic use.

Start with one small change

Living more sustainably does not have to happen all at once. Choose one habit you can keep doing, share it with someone, and build from there.

  • Carry a reusable bag and water bottle.
  • Choose products with less packaging when possible.
  • Reuse and recycle materials according to local guidelines.
Make it a community effort.

Invite a friend or neighbor to join you. Small actions become more powerful when we do them together.
